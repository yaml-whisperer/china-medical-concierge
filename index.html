What we arrange

Facilitation services around hospital matching and on-the-ground coordination — not medical treatment.

1. Hospital matching

Introductions to suitable hospitals / international departments based on your case summary.

2. Interpreter & escort

3. Arrival logistics

Guidance on documents, timing, and local coordination (partners as needed).

4. Insurance / payment coordination

Help clarifying what hospitals accept and how fees are typically paid (no payment guarantees).

5. Case coordination

One point of contact from first inquiry to visit day.

No. We do not promise medical outcomes.
